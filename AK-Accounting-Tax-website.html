Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Do you work with individuals or just businesses?+

Both. A lot of people start with a personal tax return and bring over a small business or self-employed income once they see how the process works.

How often do you handle bookkeeping?+

Weekly, monthly, or quarterly, whatever matches the size and pace of your business. We'll recommend a cadence during the consultation.

What do I need to bring to a first consultation?+

Just your most recent tax return or financial statements if you have them. Nothing needs to be organized beforehand, that's our job.

Can you help if my books are behind by a year or more?+

Yes. Catch-up bookkeeping is a normal starting point for a lot of clients. We'll work through prior periods systematically to get you current.

You're a newer practice, why should I trust you with my finances?+

Since starting my business in 2006, I have been helping clients with their accounting, bookkeeping, and tax needs. Before becoming an entrepreneur, I spent many years working in senior management positions for large corporations, giving me valuable insight into the financial and operational challenges businesses face.
