Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 27, 2026  ·  AK Accounting & Tax, Lower Mainland, B.C.

Short version: When you fill out our contact form, your information goes directly to us — Amita Kumar at AK Accounting & Tax. We use it only to respond to your inquiry. We do not store it on any website server or share it with anyone. If you become a client, your financial information is handled with full professional confidentiality under applicable Canadian laws.

1. Who We Are & Who Is Responsible

AK Accounting & Tax is a private accounting and tax preparation practice based in the Lower Mainland, British Columbia, Canada. Amita Kumar is the designated person responsible for privacy at this organization. She can be reached at the contact details in Section 10 below.

This policy is governed by British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA, SBC 2003) and, where applicable, the federal Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

2. What Information We Collect

Website contact form: When you submit the consultation request form on our website, you provide your name, email address, and an optional message describing your accounting or tax needs.

As a client: If you engage our services, we collect additional information necessary to prepare your tax returns or financial statements — including, but not limited to, your Social Insurance Number (SIN), income documents (T4s, T5s, invoices), banking details, and business financial records. This information is collected directly from you and is used solely to provide the accounting or tax services you have requested.

3. Purpose of Collection

We collect personal information for the following specific purposes:

(a) To respond to your initial inquiry and schedule a free consultation.
(b) To prepare personal or corporate tax returns, bookkeeping records, and year-end financial statements on your behalf.
(c) To comply with requirements set by the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) and other applicable regulatory bodies.

We do not use your information for marketing, newsletters, or automated outreach. If we ever wish to contact you for promotional purposes, we will obtain your separate, express consent as required by Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL).

4. Consent

By submitting the contact form on our website, you consent to us collecting and using your information to respond to your inquiry. For clients, your consent to collect financial and personal information is obtained at the start of our engagement and applies to the specific services agreed upon.

You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us, subject to legal or contractual obligations that may require us to retain certain records.

5. How Your Website Data Is Transmitted

Website form submissions are sent directly to our business email. We do not operate a backend database or web server that stores your form submission. Your details travel from your browser to our inbox — nothing is retained on any web server.

Google Fonts: This website loads fonts from Google's servers (fonts.googleapis.com). When your browser requests these fonts, Google may log your IP address. This is a standard practice for web font delivery. We do not control or access this data. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy at google.com/policies/privacy.

6. Professional Confidentiality & Sensitive Data

Important for clients: As a tax and accounting practice, we handle sensitive personal and financial information including SINs, income records, and business financials. This information is treated as strictly confidential. We are bound by professional ethical obligations under Canadian accounting standards and CRA regulations. Client financial files are retained for a minimum of seven (7) years in accordance with CRA record-keeping requirements.

Client files are stored securely. Physical documents are kept in a locked environment. Digital files are protected by passwords and access controls. We do not share client financial information with any third party unless required by law (e.g., CRA audit request) or explicitly authorized by you in writing.

7. Limiting Collection

We collect only the personal information necessary for the stated purposes. We do not collect information beyond what is needed to provide our services or respond to your inquiry.

8. Retention

Website inquiries: Contact form emails are retained only as long as necessary to complete the follow-up. If no engagement results, we delete your details within 90 days.

Client records: Financial records and tax filings are retained for a minimum of seven (7) years as required by the Canada Revenue Agency. After this period, records are securely destroyed.

9. Security Safeguards

We use security measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the information:

  • Website form data is transmitted over HTTPS (encrypted in transit).
  • Email containing client information is protected by password-controlled email accounts.
  • Physical documents are stored in a locked, access-controlled environment.
  • Digital client files are protected by password access and stored on secured devices.

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We take reasonable precautions to protect your information but cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.

We may disclose your information to the Canada Revenue Agency, provincial tax authorities, or other regulatory bodies when required to do so by law, or when you explicitly authorize us to act on your behalf.

11. Your Rights Under PIPA BC

Under British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we hold about you.
  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request corrections if information is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Withdraw consent for non-essential uses.
  • Make a complaint about how we handle your information.

To exercise any of these rights, contact Amita Kumar using the details in Section 12 below. We will respond within 30 days.
